Welcome to My Website

This website explains how Management Information Systems help businesses organize information and make decisions. It also explores artificial intelligence and how people can use it responsibly. MIS connects people, technology, and data to support business goals. You can learn more about technology through Gartner, MIT Technology Review, and CISA. The website also explains cybersecurity and ways to protect information. Understanding these topics helps people make responsible choices when using technology.

This website introduces important topics in Management Information Systems, including artificial intelligence, data, and cybersecurity. It explains how technology helps organizations manage information and make better decisions. The AI Usage page discusses responsible and dishonest uses of AI. The Security page explains malware and cyber threats. The About Us page shares what I learned from this assignment. Together, these topics show why technology and responsibility are important in school and the workplace.
