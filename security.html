Malware and Cyber Threats

The WannaCry Ransomware Attack

The WannaCry ransomware attack began on May 12, 2017. It infected computers in many countries and encrypted files, preventing people from accessing their information. The attackers demanded payment to unlock the files. The incident was significant because it disrupted organizations, including healthcare services, and showed the importance of security updates and backups. Ransomware is malware that locks or encrypts files and demands payment.

Learn more from the Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency (CISA) .

Types of Malware

  1. Ransomware: locks or encrypts files and demands payment.
  2. Trojan: pretends to be legitimate software to trick users.

Common Hacker Motives

  1. Financial gain through theft or extortion.
  2. Political or social goals, sometimes called hacktivism.

Prevention Strategies

  • Use multi-factor authentication.
  • Keep software updated and use security protection.
